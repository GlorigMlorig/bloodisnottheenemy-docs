Account & Telemetry Deletion Portal

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play Account Deletion mandates, and GDPR/CCPA privacy rights, all users possess the unconditional right to erase their account, partner bonds, and cycle telemetry.

Method 1: Instant In-App Self-Serve Purge (Recommended)

If you have the Blood Is Not The Enemy application installed on iOS, Android, or Web:

  1. Open Blood Is Not The Enemy on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (Gear icon in the top header).
  3. Scroll down to the Permanent Telemetry Purge section.
  4. Tap the red action button: "Purge All Data & Terminate Account".
  5. Confirm the confirmation prompt.

What Is Erased Immediately:

  • All cloud-stored menstrual telemetry, cycle lengths, and historical period start dates.
  • All comfort flares, distress signals, and responder notes.
  • All observation notes, pain tags, and personal partner aliases.
  • The cryptographic bond linking Hearth and Flamebearer.
  • Device push notification registration tokens.
  • The AWS Cognito authentication user record and local sandbox cache.
