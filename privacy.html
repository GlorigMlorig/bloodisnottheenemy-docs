The Sanctuary Covenant

Last Updated: September 2026 // Blood Is Not The Enemy

Zero Commercial Exploitation Covenant: We honor bodily sovereignty. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your menstrual health data, cycle records, or partner telemetry to third-party data brokers, ad networks, or pharmaceutical entities. Period.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Blood Is Not The Enemy is an atmospheric menstrual cycle telemetry and partner care companion. We reject predatory tracking, manipulative ad engines, and pastel clichés. All telemetry logged exists exclusively to provide cycle phase forecasting, distress flare dispatch, and protective partner care between paired nodes (Flamebearer and Hearth).

2. Telemetry We Collect & Why

A. Reproductive & Menstrual Health Data

  • Cycle Telemetry: Date of period onset, historical bleed durations, and average cycle length. Used strictly to compute deterministic alchemical lunar phases (Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory, Luteal) on-device.
  • Comfort Flares: Real-time comfort distress signals dispatched from Hearth (Cauldron & Heat, Dark Fuel, The Torment, Radio Silence, Coven Comfort) and partner acknowledgments ("On It 🖤").
  • Daily Care Checklist: Completion status of phase-specific care directives (e.g., preparing compresses, hydration, apothecary stocking).
  • Observation Notes: Private journal entries, physical symptoms, and pain tags logged by users.

B. Identifiers & Device Telemetry

  • Account Authentication: Email address and Cognito user identifier (`sub`) upon sign-in. Used solely to secure your cryptographic orbital bond.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Native Apple Push (APNs) and Firebase (FCM) device tokens used exclusively to alert Flamebearers to incoming comfort flares.
  • Device State: Local preference cache (haptics, theme preference).

3. Security Architecture & Data Isolation

Your records are protected by strict row-level security:

  • Local-First Storage: Telemetry is stored locally on your device in sandboxed encrypted storage, enabling full offline operation.
  • Owner-Based Cloud Authorization: Cloud data in AWS AppSync and DynamoDB is isolated with owner-based row security (`allow.owner()`). Only you and your explicitly bonded partner can access your cycle signals.
  • Transport Security: 100% of network communication is encrypted over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.

4. Complete Account & Telemetry Purge

We respect your absolute right to be forgotten (complying with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play Account Deletion policies, and GDPR):

  • Instant In-App Purge: You may wipe all cloud data, pairing bonds, and local storage in one tap inside the app: navigate to Settings → Permanent Telemetry Purge → Purge All Data & Terminate Account.
  • External Web Deletion: If you uninstalled the application without purging, visit our dedicated Account Deletion Portal to submit an erasure directive.
